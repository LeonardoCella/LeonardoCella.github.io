About me

I work at the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and energy trading. Since September 2026, I have been Chief Scientist Officer (CSO) at an energy trading startup now taking shape.

I also joined AIPIA’s Technical and Scientific Committee in September 2026.

September 2026

A new chapter in energy trading

I am excited to help build this venture alongside highly experienced colleagues with outstanding track records in the industry.

We are building a trading approach grounded entirely in AI and quantitative methods, including AI agents. Our primary focus is the Nordic power markets, with ambitions that extend beyond imbalance trading. For me, this is a demanding and exhilarating challenge. Let the new adventure begin!

A heartfelt thank you to Duferco Energia and the colleagues who made my time there so rewarding.

Research interests

I use mathematics to model and understand the physical world. I am interested in how real-world events unfold, how their patterns can be captured in models, and how those models can inform decisions under uncertainty.

In energy markets, this means connecting physical dynamics to price behaviour and trading decisions through statistics, machine learning, and reinforcement learning.

Background

Before this venture, I worked at Duferco Energia. Earlier, I spent nearly two years in the Energy Portfolio team at Axpo Italia, focusing on customer consumption and the intraday power market.

At the Italian Institute of Technology, I worked with Massimiliano Pontil on multi-task learning, meta-learning, and stochastic linear bandits. This research also involved Karim Lounici. I collaborated with Claudio Gentile, then at Google in New York, on non-stationary stochastic bandits.

I hold a PhD from the University of Milan, where I was advised by Nicolò Cesa-Bianchi. From July to October 2018, I was an Applied Scientist intern in Amazon’s Music ML team in Berlin, mentored by Giovanni Zappella.

Since 2018, I have served on programme committees for machine learning conferences including AISTATS, NeurIPS, and ICML.
